よくある質問

  • Do I need an eVisa to travel?

    Visa rules depend on your nationality and destination. Many countries allow ビザ免除 entry, while others require an online travel authorization (eVisa, ETA, ESTA) or a traditional embassy visa. Use our destination guides to check your specific situation.

  • How long does an eVisa take to be approved?

    Processing ranges from minutes (ESTA, Canada eTA) to several business days (India, Thailand). Always apply at least 10 days before travel.

  • What documents are needed for an online visa?

    Most eVisa applications require a valid passport (6+ months), a recent photo, return flight details, proof of accommodation, and a payment method. Requirements vary by destination.
