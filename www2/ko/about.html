About eVisa-Card.com

Your trusted free guide to eVisas, travel authorizations and visa requirements worldwide.

What is eVisa-Card.com?

eVisa-Card.com is an independent travel information platform helping travelers understand visa requirements, online application processes and travel authorizations for countries around the world.

We compile official eVisa information : including visa types, durations, fees, eligibility criteria and step-by-step application guides : so you can plan your trip with confidence.

Our goal is to make international travel more accessible by providing clear, up-to-date visa guidance at no cost.
