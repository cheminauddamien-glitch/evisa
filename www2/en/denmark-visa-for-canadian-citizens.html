Do Canadian Citizens Need a Visa for Denmark?

No. Canadian passport holders are visa-exempt for short stays in Denmark and the entire Schengen Area. However, ETIAS (European Travel Information and Authorisation System) is required before travel.

ETIAS : What You Need to Know

ETIAS is an electronic pre-travel authorisation (not a visa). You apply online, pay €20, and receive approval within minutes in most cases. ETIAS is valid for 3 years or until your passport expires, whichever comes first, and covers unlimited trips across all Schengen countries.

Entry Requirements for Canadian Citizens

  • Valid Canadian passport (at least 3 months beyond planned stay)
  • Valid ETIAS authorisation (obtained before departure)
  • Proof of sufficient funds (approx. €100/day)
  • Return or onward travel ticket
  • Travel/health insurance recommended

Schengen 90/180 Day Rule

The 90-day limit applies across the entire Schengen Area, not just Denmark. Time spent in any Schengen country counts toward your 90-day allowance in any rolling 180-day period.

Cost & Validity for Canadian Travellers

Canadian citizens pay no visa fee to enter Denmark for tourism. The permitted stay is 90 days / 180-day period (Schengen), granted automatically on arrival with a valid passport. Because entry is visa-free, there is no application form, no embassy appointment and no processing wait : entry is granted at the border.

Length of Stay & Overstaying Denmark

The standard tourist stay for Canadian citizens is 90 days / 180-day period (Schengen). Overstaying this limit in Denmark can lead to fines, deportation and a temporary re-entry ban, so track your permitted days carefully. If you need longer, enquire about an extension or a different visa category with Denmark’s immigration authority before your authorised stay expires.
