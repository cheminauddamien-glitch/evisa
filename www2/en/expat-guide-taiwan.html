Expat Guide to Taiwan 2026: Employment Gold Card, Taipei Living and National Health Insurance

Taiwan has quietly become one of Asia's most practical relocation destinations. The Employment Gold Card bundles a resident visa, an open work permit and an ARC into one application, and National Health Insurance covers residents for a premium most Western expats find hard to believe.

This guide covers what applies in September 2026: visa-free entry, the Gold Card's NT$160,000 salary route, the two-year digital nomad visa, work permits, the ARC-to-APRC path, NHI timing, tax bands and real Taipei rents.

Taiwan is outside the Schengen Area and unaffected by ETIAS or the EU Entry/Exit System.

Who Taiwan Is Best For

Taiwan rewards a specific profile: well-paid knowledge workers wanting Asia without Singapore or Hong Kong prices. It is the wrong island for a cheap retirement.

ProfileFit
Tech and semiconductor prosExcellent — Hsinchu Science Park, TSMC, MediaTek.
High-earning remote workersExcellent — Gold Card salary route needs NT$160,000/month; income earned abroad qualifies.
FamiliesVery good — NHI covers dependants, established international schools, exceptional safety.
Digital nomads, modest incomeGood — nomad visa runs to two years but gives no ARC or NHI.
RetireesWeak — no retirement visa; need a family tie or a Gold Card qualification.
Good to know: Taiwan uses traditional Chinese characters, not the mainland's simplified set; spoken Mandarin transfers directly.

Visa and Residency Routes

Short stays are easy for most nationalities. Long stays run through a resident visa plus an ARC; the Gold Card compresses several steps into one application.

RouteRules
Visa-exempt, 90 daysUS, UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, Japan, South Korea, Israel, Chile, Paraguay, all EU/EFTA. No extension; passport valid 6+ months.
Visa-exempt, 30 daysSingapore, Malaysia and five Caribbean states.
Visa-exempt, 14 daysThailand, Brunei, Philippines and Oman, trial to 31 July 2027.
eVisa20 nationalities incl. Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Mauritius, Peru, Saudi Arabia, Turkiye, UAE. Single entry, 3-month validity, 30-day stay.
Employment Gold Card11 fields; bundles resident visa, open work permit, ARC and re-entry permit. 1-3 years, NT$3,700-5,700. Over 60 business days.
Employer work permitSpecialist hire; salary NT$47,971+/month; master's, or bachelor's plus 2+ years.
Digital Nomad Visitor Visa6 months, extendable to 2 years since Jan 2026. No ARC, no NHI.
ARC / APRCARC: 1-3 years. APRC: 5 years' residence, or 3 for Gold Card professionals (1 if Taiwan income reaches NT$6,000,000). Fee NT$10,000.

The Gold Card's Economy field salary test needs NT$160,000/month before tax, shown on a tax statement, contract or employer certification — income earned anywhere counts. Seven other routes cover doctorates, executives and specialists with eight-plus years' experience.

  1. Confirm you meet one qualification item and gather the evidence.
  2. Apply online via the Gold Card portal and pay the fee.
  3. Wait through joint government review — budget 60+ business days.
  4. Collect the card at a National Immigration Agency centre; it doubles as your ARC.
  5. Register, open a bank account, and enrol in NHI once eligible.
Pro Tip: Choose the three-year Gold Card: NT$2,000 more than the one-year card buys two more years without re-documenting income.
Good to know: Every arriving foreign national must complete the digital Taiwan Arrival Card online before border control.

Digital Nomads and Remote Work

Taiwan launched a six-month digital nomad visitor visa in 2025 and extended total stay to two years in January 2026: an initial six months plus three six-month extensions, reviewed by the National Development Council.

RequirementDetail
NationalityVisa-exempt passport, or an existing nomad visa from another country.
IncomeUSD 40,000/year if 30+; USD 20,000 if 20-29.
SavingsSix months averaging USD 10,000+.
LimitsNo ARC, no NHI, no local employment.

Taipei suits nomads well: cheap fibre, laptop-friendly cafes, a car-free MRT, and an airport a short flight from Tokyo, Seoul and Bangkok.

Pro Tip: If you clear the Gold Card's salary test, take the Gold Card instead — it costs less over three years and adds NHI, tax residency and time towards permanent residence.

Working Locally

Taiwan's economy runs on hardware: semiconductors and precision machinery drive exports and salaries, concentrated around Hsinchu Science Park.

  • Employment Gold Card: open work permit — work for anyone, freelance, or start a company without a sponsor.
  • Employer-sponsored permit: employer applies via the Workforce Development Agency; salary NT$47,971+/month, degree or equivalent experience required.
  • Teaching English: a large market via cram schools and public schools.
  • Gold Card spouses: since 2026, can apply for their own open work permit.

Engineering pay is good regionally but below Silicon Valley; low tax and cheap healthcare compensate.

Good to know: Working locally on a visitor visa, including the nomad visa, is not permitted — only remote work for a foreign employer.

Retirement in Taiwan

Taiwan's weakest area: no retirement visa, no passive-income visa, no pension-based residence.

OptionHow it works
Employment Gold CardNo age barrier; the salary test can be met against past earnings and does not require working in Taiwan.
Family routeParents, spouses and dependants of ROC nationals or ARC holders — how most retirees are here.
Investment residenceExists but conditions change; check the National Immigration Agency first.

The reward for those who settle: NHI is exceptional value and living costs outside central Taipei are low.

Family and Dependants

Families are treated well once the principal applicant has resident status: dependants get their own linked ARC and NHI cover.

  • Spouse: dependent ARC; since 2026, Gold Card spouses may apply for their own open work permit.
  • Children: dependent ARC, access to public or international school.
  • Parents: a Gold Card holder's direct-line relatives may qualify for an extended-stay visitor visa.
  • Marriage: a foreign certificate must be authenticated abroad before registration.
Pro Tip: Register every family member's address in one visit — it unlocks schools, NHI cards and bank accounts together.

Healthcare and National Health Insurance

NHI is a single-payer system covering outpatient care, hospital treatment, dentistry and prescriptions, with the insured card accepted almost everywhere.

SituationEnrolment
Employed by a Taiwanese employerFrom day one, through the employer.
ARC holder, not employed locallyAfter six months' continuous residence.
Digital nomad visa holderNot eligible — private insurance is essential.

The 2026 premium rate is 5.17 per cent of insured payroll, unchanged from 2025. A self-paying insured person pays a flat NT$826/month, plus a co-payment that rises with facility level.

Good to know: Bring your NHI card to every appointment; without it, hospitals charge the full self-pay rate.

Private and International Health Insurance

Most arrivals need private cover for the six months before NHI eligibility, or for a whole nomad-visa stay; NHI excludes private rooms and most elective treatment.

  • The six-month gap: an international policy (Cigna Global, Allianz Care, AXA) is what most Gold Card arrivals buy.
  • Nomad visa holders: no NHI at all, so full cover is not optional.
  • After enrolment: a light local top-up usually beats a full international plan; out-of-pocket costs in Taiwan are low.

Banking

Opening an account is straightforward with an ARC, awkward without one; expect a paper-heavy, branch-based appointment.

ItemDetail
DocumentsPassport, ARC, local mobile number, proof of address.
Foreigner-friendly banksBank of Taiwan, CTBC, E.SUN, Taishin, Cathay United.
Without an ARCOnly a restricted account; many branches decline.
Pro Tip: Get a Taiwanese SIM before your bank appointment — most banks use SMS two-factor authentication.

Tax Residency and Income Tax

Taiwan taxes residents on Taiwan-source income; the threshold is 183 days a calendar year. Below that, withholding applies; filing season is May, via the Ministry of Finance eTax portal.

Net taxable income (NT$)Rate
Up to 610,0005%
610,001 - 1,380,00012%
1,380,001 - 2,770,00020%
2,770,001 - 5,190,00030%
Over 5,190,00040%

For 2026 a single employed resident can claim NT$101,000 exemption, NT$136,000 standard deduction and NT$227,000 salary deduction — NT$464,000 before tax is due.

The Gold Card's quiet advantage: a foreign special professional resident 183+ days, earning above NT$3,000,000 a year, may exclude half the salary above that threshold for five years. You must apply and hold a Gold Card or specialist permit from the start of employment.

Good to know: ETIAS is still not in force as of September 2026 and has no bearing on Taiwan. Consult a Taiwanese CPA before restructuring your affairs.

Housing: Renting and Buying

Renting

Leases run one year as standard; deposits are one to two months' rent, agency fees about the same.

2BR, central Taipei40,000-70,000
2BR, outer Taipei / Hsinchu20,000-40,000

Good to know: Rent figures are indicative editorial estimates for September 2026.

Buying

Foreigners can own property only on reciprocity: Article 18 of the Land Act permits acquisition where the buyer's country grants ROC nationals the same right. The Interior Ministry lists reciprocal nations; otherwise a reciprocity certificate is required first. A mortgage normally needs an ARC and local income.

Pro Tip: Renting the first two or three years usually costs less than the taxes on a quick purchase and resale, since Taipei yields are low relative to price.

Transport and Driving Licence

Taiwan has one of Asia's best public transport networks; in Taipei a car is a liability. The EasyCard works on the MRT, buses, trains, ferries and YouBike.

  • Taipei MRT and TPASS: clean, punctual, signed in English; the pass covers MRT, buses and YouBike.
  • High Speed Rail: Taipei to Kaohsiung in around 100 minutes.
  • Driving licence: reciprocity-based — some states convert Taiwanese licences without a test.
Pro Tip: Check the Highway Bureau's reciprocity table for your exact state or province — it is often decided sub-nationally.

Schools and Education

Taipei has a mature international school sector and a growing bilingual public offering; international schools are expensive, bilingual public schools far cheaper.

  • Taipei American School and Taipei European School are the largest, offering American, British, German, French and IB tracks.
  • Public school: ARC-holding children can enrol; Mandarin suits younger children better than arriving teenagers.
Good to know: Places at the largest international schools are genuinely constrained at some year levels — apply early.

Phone and Internet

Connectivity is a clear win: fast, cheap fibre and generous mobile data.

  • On arrival: a prepaid SIM or eSIM at Taoyuan or Songshan airport needs only a passport.
  • Postpaid contracts: require an ARC and usually a local bank account.

Safety and Emergency Numbers

Taiwan is one of the safest places in the world day to day; the real risks are natural — typhoons, earthquakes and summer heat.

Moving Checklist

Before You Leave

  1. Gather evidence for your route: tax statements, bank statements, or an employer offer.
  2. Confirm 6+ months' passport validity from your entry date.
  3. Apply for the Gold Card early — over 60 business days is normal.
  4. Buy international health insurance for at least your first six months.

First Month in Taiwan

  1. Complete the Taiwan Arrival Card online before border control.
  2. Collect your Gold Card or apply for your ARC at a National Immigration Agency centre.
  3. Register your address, open a bank account, buy a SIM and an EasyCard.
  4. Enrol in NHI through your employer immediately, or diarise your six-month eligibility date.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can I stay in Taiwan without a visa?

US, UK, Canadian, Australian, Japanese, South Korean and EU/EFTA passport holders get 90 days. Singapore and Malaysia get 30 days; Thailand, Brunei, the Philippines and Oman get 14 days under a trial to 31 July 2027. Everyone completes the Arrival Card online first.

What salary do I need for the Taiwan Employment Gold Card?

The Economy field salary route needs NT$160,000/month before tax, shown on a tax statement, contract or employer certification — income earned anywhere counts. Ten other fields use qualification lists based on awards or experience instead.

Does the Employment Gold Card include a work permit?

Yes — it bundles a resident visa, an open work permit, an ARC and a re-entry permit, so you can work for anyone without a sponsor. One to three years costs NT$3,700-5,700; processing usually exceeds 60 business days.

How does the Taiwan digital nomad visa work in 2026?

A visitor visa for six months, extendable to two years since January 2026. You need a visa-exempt-eligible passport, income of USD 40,000 (30+) or USD 20,000 (20-29), and six months of bank statements averaging USD 10,000. No ARC and no NHI.

When can I join Taiwan's National Health Insurance and what does it cost?

Employed locally, enrolment starts day one. Otherwise you need an ARC plus six months' residence. The 2026 rate is 5.17 per cent of insured payroll; self-paying insured persons pay a flat NT$826 a month.

Am I a Taiwanese tax resident and what will I pay?

You are resident once you spend 183+ days in Taiwan a calendar year. 2026 rates run 5 per cent up to NT$610,000, rising to 40 per cent above NT$5,190,000. Foreign professionals earning over NT$3,000,000 can exclude half the excess for five years, if they apply.

Can foreigners buy property in Taiwan?

Only on reciprocity: Article 18 of the Land Act allows it if your country grants ROC nationals the same right. Otherwise a reciprocity certificate is needed first, and a mortgage normally requires an ARC and local income.
