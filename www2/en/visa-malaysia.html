Malaysia eVisa & Travel Information (2026)

Updated: 8 July 2026.

Important 2026 update : MDAC is mandatory and FREE — beware copycat sites, and China/India visa-free entry extended

The Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC) is mandatory for all foreign visitors arriving by air, land or sea, submitted online within 3 days before arrival. It is completely FREE on the official Malaysian Immigration Department portal — beware of copycat/scam third-party websites charging a fee for this free service. Travelers using the "MDAC-Autogate" e-gates at major checkpoints get processed in as little as ~20 seconds. Only Singaporean citizens, Malaysian permanent residents, and diplomatic passport holders are exempt from the MDAC. Separately, the 30-day visa-free entry facility for Chinese and Indian nationals has been extended through 31 December 2026.

Effective: MDAC in effect since December 2023; China/India visa-free facility extended through 31 December 2026.

Source: Immigration Department of Malaysia (imi.gov.my) / MDAC official portal (imigresen-online.imi.gov.my)

eVisa Available

Many nationalities can enter Malaysia visa-free for 14-90 days. Chinese and Indian nationals benefit from a 30-day visa-free entry facility extended through 31 December 2026. For those still requiring a visa, an eVisa can be obtained online.

Introduction

Malaysia is a multicultural Southeast Asian nation known for its rainforests, Bornean wildlife, the Petronas Twin Towers, stunning Langkawi islands and diverse cuisine (nasi lemak, roti canai, char kway teow). Main airports are Kuala Lumpur International (KUL/KLIA), Kuala Lumpur KLIA2 (KUL), Penang (PEN) and Kota Kinabalu (BKI). The Immigration Department of Malaysia (Jabatan Imigresen Malaysia) manages entry and visas.

Malaysia is one of the most visa-liberal countries in Asia: citizens of most Western countries, Australia, Japan, South Korea, New Zealand, most ASEAN nations and many others receive 30-90 days visa-free entry. Malaysia uses a Social Visit Pass system for visitors.

Tourist & Short Stay

Most nationalities receive a Social Visit Pass (eVP) of 30, 60, or 90 days stamped at the port of entry. USA, UK, Canada, Australia, EU nations, Japan and most others receive 90 days automatically at immigration. ASEAN nationals receive 30-90 days depending on bilateral agreements. No prior application is needed for visa-free nationals.

For nationalities not covered by visa-free agreements, Malaysia offers an eVisa (online application) and Visa on Arrival at Kuala Lumpur International Airport for certain nationalities. Chinese and Indian nationals benefit from a dedicated 30-day visa-free entry facility, extended through 31 December 2026, which has largely superseded the older eNTRI short-stay registration for tourism purposes.

Business Visa

Business visits are covered by the Social Visit Pass for most nationalities. For extended business stays or employment, a Professional Visit Pass or Employment Pass (issued by Immigration Malaysia) is required. The employer applies on the worker's behalf.

Work & Long-Stay

  • Employment Pass (EP) : for skilled workers earning MYR 5,000+/month; categories 1-3 based on salary and contract duration.
  • Residence Pass-Talent (RP-T) : for high-performing professionals who have held an Employment Pass; allows indefinite stay without employer sponsorship and free to work for any Malaysian company.
  • Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) : long-term residency visa for retirees and wealthy individuals; revised requirements since 2021 (offshore income ≥MYR 40,000/month, fixed deposit MYR 1M, minimum property purchase MYR 2M, etc.).
  • DE Rantau (Digital Nomad) : Malaysia launched DE Rantau in 2022 for digital nomads with tech-related employment; valid 3-12 months.

Student Pass

International students need a Student Pass issued by Immigration Malaysia. The university applies on the student's behalf after admission. Required: offer letter, health check, and financial proof. Student passes are typically valid for the duration of the study program.

Airport Transit

Most travelers can transit through Kuala Lumpur KLIA without a visa if remaining in the international zone. Some nationalities need a transit visa. Check the current list at imi.gov.my.

Who Is Visa-Exempt?

Citizens of USA, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, EU member states, most ASEAN nations, and many others receive visa-free entry for 30-90 days. Full list at imi.gov.my.

Health & Entry Requirements for Malaysia

No mandatory vaccinations for most travelers. Yellow Fever certificate required if arriving from endemic area. Medical exam required for Employment Pass and MM2H: chest X-ray, blood tests (HIV, Hepatitis B, VDRL). Foreign workers must register with FOMEMA. MDAC (Malaysia Digital Arrival Card) mandatory for all foreign arrivals - free online form (Singaporean citizens, Malaysian permanent residents and diplomatic passport holders exempt); beware of copycat sites charging a fee.

Visa Extension & Overstay Rules in Malaysia

Social Visit Pass 90-day stay generally not extendable for visa-exempt nationals. Employment Pass renewal through employer 3 months before expiry. MM2H renewable. Overstay: compound fine MYR 500+, detention at immigration depot, whipping (caning) possible for illegal workers, 5-year entry ban. Malaysia takes overstay very seriously.

Visa Refusal & Appeals for Malaysia

Common reasons: suspicious travel patterns, inadequate funds, previous immigration violations, blacklisted. No formal appeal for entry refusal. eVISA rejections can be contested through VLN Global. Reapplication possible after 3 months.

How to get the Malaysia visa?

  1. Check whether you need a visa. Confirm your nationality's requirement for Malaysia in the table above (visa-free, eVisa, visa on arrival or consular visa).
  2. Choose the right visa type for your trip : tourism, business, transit, work or study.
  3. Prepare your documents: a passport valid at least 6 months, a recent photo, proof of accommodation and onward travel, and sufficient funds.
  4. Apply online (eVisa) or at the nearest Malaysia embassy/consulate, and pay the visa fee.
  5. Receive your visa and print or save the approval before you travel to Malaysia.
