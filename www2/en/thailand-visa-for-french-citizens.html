Thailand Visa for French Citizens 2026

Updated: 20 May 2026.

Important 2026 update : Thailand visa exemption reform : May 2026

The Thai Cabinet decided on 19 May 2026 to cancel the 60-day visa-free scheme. The new policy introduces a 30-day visa exemption for 54 countries (Australia, Austria, Canada, France, Germany, Japan, New Zealand, Singapore, UK, US, etc.) and a 15-day exemption for 3 countries. The Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) remains mandatory and replaces the paper TM6. Effective: Cabinet decision 19 May 2026 : rollout mid-2026. Source: Thai MFA / Royal Gazette

Do French Citizens Need a Visa for Thailand?

French citizens do not need a visa to visit Thailand for tourism or short business trips. Thailand grants visa-free entry for up to 60 days (extended from 30 days in November 2024). No pre-arrival authorization is required : simply arrive with a valid passport.

2026 Update: The Thai Cabinet decided on 19 May 2026 to revert to a 30-day visa exemption for French citizens (among 54 nationalities). Check the latest rules before travel.

How to Apply

No application is needed for stays up to the permitted visa-free period. On arrival, present your valid passport (6+ months validity), a return or onward ticket, and proof of accommodation. For stays beyond the visa-free period, apply for a Tourist Visa (TR) at thaievisa.go.th before departure.

Cost & Validity for French Travellers

French citizens pay no visa fee to enter Thailand for tourism. Entry is granted automatically on arrival with a valid passport. Because entry is visa-free, there is no application form, no embassy appointment and no processing wait.

Length of Stay & Overstaying Thailand

The standard tourist stay for French citizens is currently 60 days (subject to change in mid-2026). Overstaying this limit in Thailand can lead to fines, deportation and a temporary re-entry ban. Track your permitted days carefully. If you need longer, visit a Thai Immigration Office for an extension (fee: THB 1,900 for 30 days).

Tips for French Travelers

  • Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months beyond your intended stay.
  • Carry a printed return or onward ticket : immigration officers may ask for it on arrival.
  • Keep THB 10,000 per person (or equivalent) in accessible funds.
  • Travel insurance is not mandatory but strongly recommended.
  • To extend your stay, visit any Thai Immigration Office.
  • Complete the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) before arrival : it replaces the old paper TM6.

Important disclaimer

Visa rules change frequently. Always verify current requirements at consular.mfa.go.th before booking travel. This page is for informational purposes only.
