Overview of Denmark Visa Fees in 2026

As a Schengen Area member, Denmark applies the standardised Schengen uniform visa fee of EUR 80 for short-stay visas (Type C). This fee is set at the EU level and applies uniformly across all Schengen member states. In addition to the government fee, applicants using a VFS Global visa application centre pay a separate service fee, typically EUR 30–50 depending on the country of application.

Understanding the total cost of your Denmark visa application is essential to budget correctly. This page provides a complete breakdown of all applicable fees, including the primary visa charge, application centre service fee, optional courier charges, and any biometric collection costs.

Detailed Fee Breakdown for Denmark Visa 2026

The primary government visa fee for Denmark is EUR 80 per adult applicant for a standard short-stay (Type C) visa. Children between 6 and 12 years pay EUR 40; children under 6 are exempt. This fee is payable at the time of application submission and is non-refundable regardless of the outcome. Always verify the current fee schedule at nyidanmark.dk before submitting your application, as fee levels are subject to annual review.

Certain nationalities may benefit from reduced or waived fees due to bilateral visa fee reciprocity or exemption agreements. Citizens of countries with which Denmark has a visa facilitation agreement may pay a reduced rate or have the fee waived entirely. Check the official embassy or VFS Global website for nationality-specific fee schedules.

Accepted Payment Methods

Denmark visa fees are generally payable at the VFS Global visa application centre by credit card (Visa, Mastercard), debit card, or cash in the local currency. Online payment portals may be available in some countries. Always use official payment channels and retain your payment receipt as evidence of fee payment. Payments made through unauthorised third-party websites are not accepted and are not refundable by the Denmark consulate.

Refund Policy

The Denmark visa application fee is non-refundable once the application has been submitted to the embassy or application centre. This applies regardless of whether the visa is approved, refused, or withdrawn after submission. VFS Global service fees are also non-refundable. If you withdraw your application before it is formally registered, contact the application centre immediately : a partial administrative refund may be possible in limited circumstances.
