Do Mexican Citizens Need a Visa for Germany?

Mexican passport holders do not need a visa to visit Germany. Germany is part of the Schengen Area and Mexican citizens can stay for up to 90 days in any 180-day period without any pre-travel authorisation. ETIAS does not apply to Mexican passport holders.

Requirements for Mexican Citizens

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity recommended)
  • Return or onward travel ticket
  • Proof of accommodation
  • Proof of sufficient funds

How to Apply

No visa or ETIAS application is needed. Simply:

  1. Ensure your passport is valid for the duration of your stay.
  2. Book your flights and accommodation.
  3. Arrive at a German port of entry and present your passport.

Tips for Mexican Travellers to Germany

  • The 90-day limit applies across the entire Schengen Area, not just Germany.
  • Carry proof of accommodation and return ticket in case of spot checks.
  • Travel insurance, while not always compulsory, is strongly recommended.
