Spain Visa for Canadian Citizens 2026

Updated: 20 May 2026.

Important 2026 update : Spain: Golden Visa abolished, ETIAS not yet in operation

ETIAS is not yet in operation. No ETIAS travel-authorisation applications are currently being accepted. The European Union will announce the exact start date several months before launch. Until ETIAS becomes operational, eligible visa-exempt travellers do not need an ETIAS authorisation for a short stay. Once the system starts, the official ETIAS application fee is expected to be €20, subject to the official EU rules in force at that time.

Do Canadian Citizens Need a Visa for Spain?

ETIAS is not yet in operation. No ETIAS travel-authorisation applications are currently being accepted. The European Union will announce the exact start date several months before launch. Until ETIAS becomes operational, eligible visa-exempt travellers do not need an ETIAS authorisation for a short stay. Once the system starts, the official ETIAS application fee is expected to be €20, subject to the official EU rules in force at that time.

Entry Requirements for Canadian Citizens

  • Valid passport (at least 6 months validity beyond your planned return date)
  • ETIAS authorisation (apply at travel-europe.europa.eu; valid 3 years or until passport expiry)
  • Return or onward travel ticket
  • Proof of accommodation for the duration of your stay
  • Proof of sufficient funds (approx. EUR 50 per day recommended)
  • Travel or health insurance (strongly recommended)

For stays beyond 90 days, contact the Spain Embassy or Consulate in your country to apply for a national long-stay visa.

Tips for Canadian Travellers to Spain in 2026

  • ETIAS is tied to your passport : renew ETIAS if you renew your passport before travel.
  • The 90-day Schengen limit is cumulative across all Schengen countries, not just Spain.
  • Keep a digital copy of your ETIAS approval email and passport data page on your phone.
  • Purchase travel insurance covering medical emergencies and trip cancellation.
  • Check the EU short-stay calculator at ec.europa.eu to track your Schengen days.
