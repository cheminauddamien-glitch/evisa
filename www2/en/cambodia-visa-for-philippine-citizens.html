Cambodia Visa-Free Entry for Philippine Citizens

Philippine passport holders enjoy visa-free entry to Cambodia under the ASEAN Free Movement Agreement. No advance visa application is needed for stays up to 30 days.

Visa-Free Entry Details

  • Fee: Free
  • Stay: Up to 30 days
  • Entry: Present valid passport at immigration
  • Basis: ASEAN bilateral agreement

How to Apply

  1. No advance application is required.
  2. Arrive at Phnom Penh, Siem Reap, or Sihanoukville international airports, or an authorised land crossing.
  3. Present your valid passport at the immigration counter.
  4. Receive a 30-day entry stamp.

Required Documents

  • Valid passport : minimum 6 months validity recommended
  • Return or onward flight ticket
  • Proof of sufficient funds
  • Hotel booking or accommodation address (recommended)

Cost & Validity for Philippine Travellers

Philippine citizens pay no visa fee to enter Cambodia for tourism. The permitted stay is 30 days, granted automatically on arrival with a valid passport. Because entry is visa-free, there is no application form, no embassy appointment and no processing wait : n/a : no visa required.

Length of Stay & Overstaying Cambodia

The standard tourist stay for Philippine citizens is 30 days. Overstaying this limit in Cambodia can lead to fines, deportation and a temporary re-entry ban, so track your permitted days carefully. If you need longer, enquire about an extension or a different visa category with Cambodia’s immigration authority before your authorised stay expires.
