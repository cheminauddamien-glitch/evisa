Introduction

Oman is one of the safest countries in the Middle East and increasingly popular with tourists. Main entry points are Muscat International Airport (MCT) and Salalah Airport (SLL). The Royal Oman Police manages immigration and visa affairs. The eVisa system (evisa.rop.gov.om) is the primary route for most visitors.

Oman eVisa

Citizens of 100+ countries can apply online for an Oman eVisa. Options include: 10-day single entry (OMR 20 ~USD 52), 30-day single entry (OMR 20), and 1-year multiple-entry (OMR 50 ~USD 130). Processing is usually instant to 3 business days. The eVisa is sent by email as a PDF to present on arrival.

Visa-Free Entry

GCC nationals (Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE) enter visa-free. Citizens of many countries also receive visa on arrival. Some nationalities (US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea, New Zealand, Singapore) previously received free visa on arrival : check the current list at the Royal Oman Police website as policies change.

Required Documents

  • Passport-sized photo
  • Return or onward ticket
  • Accommodation booking
  • Sufficient funds

🏥 Health & Entry Requirements for Oman

Medical exam mandatory for work visa: HIV, Hepatitis B/C, TB, pregnancy test for women. Must be done at approved clinics. No vaccinations required for tourists. COVID-19 not required.

🕑 Visa Extension & Overstay Rules in Oman

Tourist visa extendable once at ROP for additional 30 days. Overstay: OMR 10/day fine. Significant overstay: detention, deportation, 2-year entry ban.

❌ Visa Refusal & Appeals for Oman

May occur for single women under 30 (some nationalities). Security screening for certain nationalities. No formal appeal.

How to get the Oman visa?

  1. Check whether you need a visa. Confirm your nationality's requirement for Oman in the table above (visa-free, eVisa, visa on arrival or consular visa).
  2. Choose the right visa type for your trip : tourism, business, transit, work or study.
  3. Prepare your documents: a passport valid at least 6 months, a recent photo, proof of accommodation and onward travel, and sufficient funds.
  4. Apply online (eVisa) or at the nearest Oman embassy/consulate, and pay the visa fee.
  5. Receive your visa and print or save the approval before you travel to Oman.
