Privacy Policy

Who we are

eVisa-Card.com is an independent informational website about visas, eVisas and travel authorizations. It is not affiliated with any government. Contact: contact@evisa-card.com.

What data we process

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect names, passport numbers or application data. The site processes only:

  • Audience measurement — via Google Tag Manager. Analytics storage is denied by default (Google Consent Mode v2) until you consent.
  • Advertising cookies — Google AdSense may set cookies to serve ads; ad storage and personalization are likewise denied by default until you consent.
  • Server logs — our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (IP address, user agent, pages requested) for security, retained for a limited period.

Legal basis and consent

Analytics and advertising storage rely on your consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Defaults are set to "denied" and are only changed by your explicit choice. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing cookies for this site in your browser.

Third parties

Google operates the measurement and advertising services used on this site. See how Google uses data from partner sites. Flag images and fonts may be loaded from third-party CDNs (flagcdn.com, jsDelivr, Google Fonts), which receive your IP address when serving files.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing. Write to contact@evisa-card.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
