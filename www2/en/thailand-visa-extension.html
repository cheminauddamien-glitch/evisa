How to Extend Your Thailand Visa in 2026

Last verified: 13 October 2026.

Since 15 September 2026, the visa exemption is 30 days instead of 60

Nationals of 60 countries and territories, including France, can enter without a visa for a tourist stay of up to 30 days; Seychelles and Mauritius qualify for 15 days. Beyond this period, or if your nationality is not exempt, you must obtain a visa before departure on the official portal thaievisa.go.th. The free TDAC must still be completed before arrival.

Sources: Thai Government · France Diplomatie (French Ministry for Europe and Foreign Affairs) · Verified on 13 October 2026.

An extension of stay in Thailand must be requested at an Immigration Bureau office, before the deadline shown on your entry stamp. It is never automatic: immigration decides whether to grant it, and for how long.

Extension of stay: overview

Submit your application before the expiry of your authorized stay, leaving some margin in case of refusal.

Can you extend your stay in Thailand?

For the TR tourist visa, the Royal Thai Embassy in France indicates that a one-month extension can be requested in Thailand, for 1,900 THB. For the 30-day visa exemption in force since 15 September 2026, the official sources consulted do not specify the extension conditions: check with the Immigration Bureau before planning a longer stay. France Diplomatie states that stays of more than 30 days require a visa obtained from a Thai consulate before departure. Leaving the country and coming back is not an extension: each new entry is subject to the decision of immigration, and visa-exempt entry by land is in principle limited to twice per calendar year.

Documents usually required

  • Valid passport with at least one blank page
  • TM.7 application form for extension of stay
  • Recent passport-style photo
  • Copies of the passport data page and the entry stamp
  • Proof of travel and accommodation
  • Official fees, depending on the situation (1,900 THB for a TR visa)

The exact list may vary from one office to another: check it on the Immigration Bureau website before you go.

Application steps

  1. Find the right office
    Go to the immigration office responsible for the place where you are staying; the list is on immigration.go.th.
  2. Prepare your file
    Complete the TM.7 form and gather the required documents and fees.
  3. Apply in time
    Go to the office during the opening hours it displays, before your permitted stay ends. Allow plenty of time: the wait can be long.
  4. Wait for the decision
    The officer reviews your application and may ask questions about your stay. The extension may be refused.
  5. Check the stamp
    If approved, the new deadline is stamped in your passport. Check it before leaving the office.

Overstaying: penalties

Warning: exceeding the authorized length of stay can have serious and lasting consequences.

Overstaying is punishable by a fine of 500 THB per day, capped at 20,000 THB. Long overstays can lead to detention, deportation and a re-entry ban of 1 to 10 years depending on the length of the overstay. Never stay beyond the date shown on your entry stamp, and do not count on an extension until it has been granted.
