Indonesia eVOA for German Citizens

German passport holders can enter Indonesia using the Electronic Visa on Arrival (eVOA). The fee is IDR 500,000 (~USD 32), the visa is valid for 30 days, and it can be extended once for a further 30 days at an Indonesian Immigration office.

eVOA Key Details

  • Fee: IDR 500,000 (~USD 32)
  • Validity: 30 days from entry
  • Extension: Once, for 30 additional days (total 60 days)
  • Entries: Single entry
  • Purpose: Tourism / short-stay

How to Apply for the Indonesia eVOA

  1. Visit molina.imigrasi.go.id.
  2. Complete the online eVOA application form with your passport details.
  3. Upload a passport photo and the passport biographical page.
  4. Pay IDR 500,000 (~USD 32) by credit/debit card.
  5. Receive an approval e-mail with a QR code (usually within minutes to 24 hours).
  6. Print or save the approval letter and present it at immigration on arrival.

Required Documents

  • Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity beyond intended stay)
  • Passport-size photo (digital)
  • Return or onward ticket
  • Proof of sufficient funds
  • Accommodation booking

Entry Points

The eVOA is accepted at all major international airports (Soekarno-Hatta, Ngurah Rai/Bali, Juanda, Kualanamu, etc.) and major seaports.

Indonesia Visa on Arrival Cost for German Citizens

The visa on arrival for German travellers costs IDR 500,000 (~USD 32) and authorises a stay of 30 days (extendable once for 30 more days). It is issued at the port of entry, so no advance application is required. Typical processing is Instant to 24 hours online, so apply with a comfortable margin before departure.

Length of Stay & Overstaying Indonesia

The standard tourist stay for German citizens is 30 days (extendable once for 30 more days). Overstaying this limit in Indonesia can lead to fines, deportation and a temporary re-entry ban, so track your permitted days carefully. If you need longer, enquire about an extension or a different visa category with Indonesia’s immigration authority before your authorised stay expires.
