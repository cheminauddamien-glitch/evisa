Thailand e-Visa : Tourist, Transit & Non-Immigrant Visa Application 2026

Updated: 8 July 2026.

Important 2026 update : Thailand visa exemption reform approved but NOT yet in effect

Thailand's Cabinet approved (19-21 May 2026) a plan to replace the 60-day visa-free scheme with a 30-day exemption for 54 countries (Australia, Austria, Canada, France, Germany, Japan, New Zealand, Singapore, UK, US, etc.) and a 15-day exemption for 3 countries (Maldives, Mauritius, Seychelles). However, as of early July 2026 this reform has NOT yet taken effect : it still requires publication in the Royal Gazette, which starts a 15-day countdown before entry into force, and that publication has not happened yet. The current rule therefore remains the 60-day visa exemption. The Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) is mandatory and replaces the paper TM6.

Effective: Not yet in effect (pending Royal Gazette publication) as of 5 August 2026 : current 60-day exemption still applies

Source: Thai MFA / Royal Gazette

The Thailand e-Visa system allows travelers to apply online for Thai visas through the official Thai e-Visa portal, eliminating the need to visit a consulate in person. The Tourist e-Visa (TR) costs THB 2,000 (approximately USD 57) and permits a 60-day stay with single entry. Other categories include Transit, Non-Immigrant B (business), Non-Immigrant O (family visit), and Non-Immigrant ED (education).

Who Needs This Visa?

Citizens of many countries can enter Thailand visa-free for 30 or 60 days depending on nationality. Travelers who wish to stay longer or who are not eligible for visa exemption must apply for an e-Visa. The e-Visa is available for applicants residing in countries where a Thai embassy or consulate provides the online service.

Application Process

  1. Visit the official Thai e-Visa portal at thaievisa.go.th.
  2. Create an account and select the visa type you need (Tourist, Transit, or Non-Immigrant).
  3. Fill in the application form with personal, passport, and travel details.
  4. Upload required documents including passport photo, passport bio page, flight itinerary, and accommodation proof.
  5. Pay the visa fee via credit or debit card.
  6. Attend a consulate appointment if required (some embassies waive this step).
  7. Receive your e-Visa approval via email and the e-Visa is electronically linked to your passport.

Required Documents

  • Valid passport with at least 6 months remaining validity and at least 1 blank page
  • Recent passport-size photograph (4x6 cm, white background)
  • Confirmed flight itinerary (round trip)
  • Proof of accommodation (hotel booking or invitation letter)
  • Proof of financial means (bank statement, minimum THB 20,000 for tourist visa)
  • Travel insurance covering the duration of stay

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Thailand e-Visa?

The Thailand e-Visa is an electronic visa application system that allows travelers to apply for Thai visas online through the official thaievisa.go.th portal, rather than visiting a Thai embassy or consulate in person.

How much does the Thailand Tourist e-Visa cost?

The Thailand Tourist e-Visa (TR category) costs THB 2,000, approximately USD 57. Transit visas cost THB 800, and Non-Immigrant visas start from THB 5,000 depending on the category.

How long can I stay in Thailand with a Tourist e-Visa?

The Thailand Tourist e-Visa allows a stay of up to 60 days. It can be extended once at a Thai Immigration office for an additional 30 days, giving a maximum stay of 90 days.

Do I still need to visit the embassy for the Thailand e-Visa?

In most cases, the Thailand e-Visa process is entirely online. However, some Thai embassies may require applicants to submit their passport in person or attend a brief appointment after the online application is approved.
