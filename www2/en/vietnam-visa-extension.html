How to Extend Your Vietnam Visa in 2026

Last verified: 13 October 2026.

Vietnam in 2026: 45-day exemption for 24 countries, e-Visa for all nationalities

Citizens of 24 countries, including France, can stay 45 days without a visa from the date of entry (Resolutions 44/NQ-CP and 229/NQ-CP); other durations apply under bilateral agreements. The e-Visa is open to all nationalities on evisa.gov.vn, for a maximum of 90 days, single or multiple entry. Official fees usually displayed: 25 USD for single entry, 50 USD for multiple entry. The pre-arrival declaration (PAI) is optional during the pilot phase and does not replace a visa.

Sources: Official Vietnam e-Visa portal · Government of Vietnam · Ministry of Public Security (PAI) · Verified on 13 October 2026.

In Vietnam, staying longer than planned is never automatic. The safest approach is to choose the right status from the outset: the exemption if 45 days are enough, otherwise an e-Visa for up to 90 days. An extension in Vietnam depends on the decision of the Immigration Department.

Can you extend your stay in Vietnam?

Resolution 44/NQ-CP grants 45 days from the date of entry and does not provide for any automatic extension: if your stay needs to last longer, apply for an e-Visa before departure. The e-Visa has a validity start date and end date; to stay beyond it, an extension or a new visa can be requested from the Immigration Department, through an organization or individual sponsoring you in Vietnam. This is never automatic and the decision rests with the authorities. Beyond 90 days, contact the Immigration Department. Beware of offers that promise a guaranteed extension.

Documents usually required

  • Valid passport
  • Valid visa, e-Visa or entry stamp
  • Application form submitted by the organization or individual sponsoring you
  • Proof of accommodation and of stay registration
  • Official fees according to the current fee schedule

The exact list depends on your situation: check it with the Immigration Department before applying.

Steps of the procedure

  1. Check your deadline
    It is shown on your entry stamp (exemption) or on your e-Visa (validity end date).
  2. Choose the right option
    If you have not left yet, apply directly for an e-Visa covering the entire length of your stay, up to 90 days.
  3. In Vietnam, go through a sponsor
    An organization or individual in Vietnam submits the application for an extension or a new visa to the Immigration Department.
  4. Wait for the decision
    The processing time and the outcome depend on the authorities. Do not stay beyond your deadline while waiting for a reply.
  5. Check the document you receive
    If approved, check the new dates before changing your return ticket.

Overstaying: penalties

Warning: exceeding the authorized length of stay can have serious and lasting consequences.

Overstaying exposes you to an administrative fine, the amount of which depends on the length of the overstay, and, in serious cases, to deportation and an entry ban. Checks are carried out on departure, at the airport. Respect the date shown on your entry stamp or on your e-Visa.
