ℹ️ Important 2026 update : Laos: new Lao Digital Immigration Form (LDIF)

The Lao Digital Immigration Form (LDIF) has been rolled out since 1 September 2025 at major airports and selected land crossings, with national deployment progressing throughout 2026. The LDIF is separate from the visa : visa rules (e-Visa USD 30-50, VoA 30 days, extension +30 days) remain unchanged.

🏥 Health & Entry Requirements for Laos

No mandatory vaccinations but Yellow Fever certificate required if coming from endemic area. Hepatitis A/B, Typhoid, Japanese Encephalitis recommended. Malaria prophylaxis recommended for rural areas. No health insurance requirement but strongly recommended. Medical facilities very limited outside Vientiane - medical evacuation to Thailand standard practice.

🕑 Visa Extension & Overstay Rules in Laos

30-day visa extendable at immigration office in Vientiane for $2/day (max 60 additional days). Overstay fine: $10/day. Significant overstays may result in detention. Immigration office: Vientiane, near Morning Market. Processing: 1-3 days for extension.

❌ Visa Refusal & Appeals for Laos

Refusals rare for tourist VOA. May occur for certain nationalities. No formal appeal. Re-entry possible after resolving issues.

How to get the Laos visa?

  1. Check whether you need a visa. Confirm your nationality's requirement for Laos in the table above (visa-free, eVisa, visa on arrival or consular visa).
  2. Choose the right visa type for your trip : tourism, business, transit, work or study.
  3. Prepare your documents: a passport valid at least 6 months, a recent photo, proof of accommodation and onward travel, and sufficient funds.
  4. Apply online (eVisa) or at the nearest Laos embassy/consulate, and pay the visa fee.
  5. Receive your visa and print or save the approval before you travel to Laos.
