Colombia Visa for Chinese Citizens 2026

Updated: 20 May 2026.

Important 2026 update : Colombia: long-stay visa financial thresholds raised +22.7%

From 1 January 2026, the financial thresholds for long-stay visas (Digital Nomad, Retirement, M and R categories) were raised by 22.7%, tracking the annual revaluation of the Colombian minimum wage. Travel health insurance is now mandatory for all visa categories. The Check-Mig form remains mandatory (72h-1h before flight). Tourist extension fee: USD 27.

Effective: 1 January 2026 (annual SMMLV indexation)

Do Chinese Citizens Need a Visa for Colombia?

Chinese passport holders must obtain a Colombian visa before travelling to Colombia. Applications are submitted online via the Cancillería (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) portal at cancilleria.gov.co. The standard visitor visa fee is USD 52 and processing typically takes 5-15 business days.

Requirements for Chinese Citizens

  • Valid passport (at least 6 months validity beyond the planned entry date; at least 2 blank pages)
  • Completed online visa application form (cancilleria.gov.co)
  • Recent passport-size photograph (3×4 cm, white background, taken within the last 6 months)
  • Bank statements for the last 3-6 months demonstrating sufficient funds
  • Round-trip flight itinerary or confirmed bookings
  • Proof of accommodation (hotel reservations, rental agreement, or host invitation letter)
  • Employment letter, proof of self-employment, or proof of student enrolment
  • Visa fee payment receipt (USD 52, paid via the Cancillería online portal)
  • Travel health insurance valid for the duration of stay in Colombia

How to Apply

  1. Create an account on the Colombian Cancillería visa portal at cancilleria.gov.co.
  2. Complete the application form : select the appropriate visa category (usually Visitor / Turismo).
  3. Upload supporting documents : scan all required documents listed above.
  4. Pay the fee : USD 52 via the online payment system (credit/debit card accepted).
  5. Submit and await decision : processing takes 5-15 business days. Check your email for updates or log in to the portal.
  6. Receive your visa : if approved, the electronic visa (digital sticker) is sent by email. Print it and carry it when travelling.

Official portal: cancilleria.gov.co

Tips for Chinese Travellers to Colombia

  • Apply at least 3-4 weeks before your travel date; processing can be slower during public holidays.
  • Ensure your travel insurance policy explicitly covers Colombia for the full duration of your stay.
  • Bank statements should reflect a minimum of USD 30-45 per day for the length of your trip.
  • The digital visa (sticker) is sent by email : download and print it before departure.
  • Colombian immigration officers may ask for proof of onward travel and accommodation at the border.
